Faith in the Midst of Uncertainty

Life does not always unfold according to our plans. Sometimes we face challenges that leave us uncertain about tomorrow. In these moments, God invites us to trust Him rather than rely entirely on our own strength.

Faith does not mean that we never experience fear. It means that even when we are afraid, we choose to believe that God remains faithful. His promises remind us that we are never alone.

Today, take a moment to surrender your worries. Trust that God is working, even when you cannot see the whole picture.

"For we walk by faith, not by sight."
— 2 Corinthians 5:7
